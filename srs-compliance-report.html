    Limitations and assumptions

    • Static review only: nothing was built, run, load-tested or clicked through for this report. Runtime-only requirements are marked Cannot Verify.
    • Audited the working tree on branch dev_ajinkya, including uncommitted fixes from the flow-audit (for example the restored sales-teams API and the password-reset pages). Those results depend on those changes being committed.
    • All 50 SRS pages were readable as text. In the requirement tables the PDF prints the IDs separately from their text; they were paired by order, which matched every table. The entity table in Section 9.2 and the Section 19 integration table are column-scrambled in the PDF and were re-aligned by reading order.
    • The SRS states no priorities. Every priority here is an auditor estimate, based on the SRS calling the nine modules and AI features the Phase 1 MVP (Section 2.6).
    • Section 1.3 objectives and Section 24 risks are not requirements and are not scored. The Section 18 report table restates Sales/Customer/Revenue/Team reports from FR-9.2–9.5; those are traced once under FR-9.x, and only the Service and Campaign reports are added as separate items.
    • Compound requirements were split where parts could differ in status (e.g. FR-2.7 into status/source, industry and owner filters). Acceptance criteria were added as AC-x items only when they add behaviour beyond their FR.
    • "Done" means the logic was read end to end (UI → API → DB) in code, not that it was observed working. Line numbers refer to the files as of the audit date.
    • Checkbox progress is stored in this browser's localStorage only. It is not shared and may be cleared by the browser.
